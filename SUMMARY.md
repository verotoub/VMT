## What the app already does (built and published)
- Stages: Available, Reserved, In the making, Ideas to propose, Sold, plus a Raw materials section.
- **Cost price is private** (hidden until "Costs hidden" is tapped). Clients only ever see the retail price.
- **Retail price = cost × 2.5, then +8.1% VAT, rounded to the nearest CHF 50.** All of this can be changed in Settings. A price set by hand is kept.
- **Who made it:** in-house (materials + hours × Javi's hourly rate, which isn't known yet) or a supplier (one cost price + the supplier's name).
- Each piece has: Mark's reference code, metal, stones, collection (Nature & Craft / Diamonds & Gold / Coloured Stones), type (including Accessories), and a **One of a kind** tick (the stone can't be found again).
- **Raw materials** have one price (what I paid). Stock goes down by itself when a piece using them is being made, is in stock, reserved or sold. It comes back if the piece goes back to being an idea or is deleted. The app warns when there isn't enough.
- **Send to client:** a picture with name, retail price and availability, plus a WhatsApp message.
- **Reminders** as delivery dates come up for pieces being made.
- **Books button:** PDFs in the style of Mark's lookbook, 4 pieces per page, with the V|T logo and never any costs:
  - **Pop-up book:** pieces available now.
  - **Our work:** sold designs, showing "Similar pieces from CHF …", or "One of a kind".
- **Mark's 184 lookbook pieces** are loaded into a "Sort them" screen (Mine / Mine, sold / Not mine). Nothing is sorted yet, and the live app has no pieces yet.
